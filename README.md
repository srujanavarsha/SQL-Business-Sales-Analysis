# SQL Business Sales Analysis

## Project Overview

A Business Analyst-style SQL project analyzing 20 retail orders to
identify revenue drivers, regional performance, order-value patterns,
and order-status performance.

## Business Problem

Management needs a clear view of sales performance across regions,
product categories, and order statuses to identify strong areas, weak
areas, and opportunities for action.

## Objectives

-   Measure total revenue and total orders.
-   Identify the highest- and lowest-performing regions and categories.
-   Analyze order performance by status.
-   Apply business thresholds with HAVING.
-   Classify orders using CASE WHEN.
-   Translate management questions into SQL.

## Tools

-   MySQL
-   MySQL Workbench
-   SQL

## Key Findings

-   Total revenue: **₹1,072,000**
-   Total orders: **20**
-   Highest-revenue region: **South --- ₹346,000**
-   Highest-revenue category: **Laptop --- ₹723,000**
-   Lowest-revenue category: **Mouse --- ₹42,000**
-   Highest regional average sales: **South --- ₹69,200**
-   Delivered orders: **15 orders / ₹960,000**

## Business Recommendations

1.  Focus growth and inventory attention on the South region.
2.  Continue prioritizing the Laptop category.
3.  Investigate pending and cancelled orders as potential revenue
    leakage.
4.  Review the Mouse category for pricing, promotion, or assortment
    opportunities.
